# Congress Trade Tape

Every stock trade members of Congress disclosed between Oct 5 and Nov 3, 2026, grouped by the day the trade was made, with sector, size, owner and how many days late it was filed. Filter by member, ticker, sector, party or buy/sell.

**Live page:** https://elwood67.github.io/congress-trades/

Data: STOCK Act filings via Capitol Trades. Sectors from Elwood's stock-universe file. Descriptive only, not trade advice.
